How Does the Otter Quotes Referral Fee Work for a Real Estate Agent?

Otter Quotes · For real estate agents · Updated November 2026

← Referral Partner Program

Otter Quotes pays real estate partners a flat referral fee, stated in one sentence: "$200 when a homeowner you refer completes a project of $10,000 or more. $50 on the same terms for referrals from partners you recruit." The fee is not a percentage of anything. It is earned when the job is complete and approved for payout. No fee is earned on a homeowner whose property is in a state the Partner Agreement lists as excluded (section A4.4).

You send a client your referral link after a roof problem comes up. A few minutes later the client asks a fair question: do you get anything for this? You will answer better if you know how the fee works before you are asked. This guide covers what earns the fee, who pays it, where to read about payment, how a client is credited to you, and what to tell your client. Section numbers below are from the Partner Agreement, version v4-2026-10.

What does a real estate partner earn, and for what?

A real estate partner earns the referral fee when a homeowner they referred completes a project of $10,000 or more and the job is approved for payout (Partner Agreement, section A4.2). The fee is a fixed dollar amount, so a larger project does not make it larger. Otter Quotes states the amount in one sentence:

$200 when a homeowner you refer completes a project of $10,000 or more. $50 on the same terms for referrals from partners you recruit.

Partner Agreement section 4 lists every fee that applies to a partner. Read it in full before you sign up.

TermWhat it means
Referral feeThe $200 in the sentence. It is earned when a homeowner you refer completes a qualifying project and the job is approved for payout.
Recruit bonusThe $50 in the sentence. It is earned on the same terms when the referral comes from a partner you recruited.
The $10,000 floorThe project must be $10,000 or more. A project of exactly $10,000 meets the floor.
FlatBoth amounts are fixed dollar amounts. Neither is a percentage of anything.
Not a discountThe agreement says every fee is paid to the partner for making a referral. No fee is a discount, rebate or credit against any price, and no fee may be described as one (section A4.6).

Partner Agreement section A4.1 also provides a $5 measurement referral fee when a homeowner you refer purchases professional measurements. It has no $10,000 floor, and no recruit bonus is paid on it (section A4.3).

The size of the project is only a test. It decides whether the floor is met. It is not what the fee is figured from.

Completion is what earns the fee. A signed contract, a booked job or a started job has not been completed, so none of them earns it on its own. The job also has to be approved for payout.

Two limits apply. No fee is earned on a homeowner in a state the Partner Agreement excludes (section A4.4). And Otter Quotes may void an unpaid fee, or offset a paid one, if the underlying job is later refunded, cancelled, charged back or reversed (section 10.1).

How does the recruit bonus work?

The recruit bonus is $50 when an agent you personally recruited refers a homeowner whose job earns a referral fee that is approved for payout (section A4.3). It is single-level, so you earn it only on agents you recruited directly, and forward-only, so it covers only referrals made after the recruiting link is in place.

Single-level means no bonus on agents your recruits recruit (section 4.1). Forward-only means no retroactive credit: if an agent was already referring clients before Otter Quotes' records linked them to you, you earn nothing on those earlier referrals. Section 9.5 adds that recruit bonuses are credited only on agents whom those records link to you as their recruiter.

Who pays the referral fee, and what does my client pay?

Otter Quotes pays the referral fee to the partner who referred the homeowner. It is not taken from your client, and you pay nothing to sign up as a partner or to refer a client. Your client's costs are separate from the fee, and Partner Agreement section 4 lists every fee a partner can earn.

  • A client who provides a copy of an insurance estimate does not need to purchase measurements.
  • A client without an insurance estimate pays an upfront charge of as little as $22.63 for the professional roof measurements that contractors use to bid, or can upload measurements they already have. That charge is refunded if the client completes a job on the platform.
  • Contractors pay Otter Quotes a fee on the accepted bid.

When is the referral fee paid?

The fee is earned when a homeowner you refer completes a qualifying job and the job is approved for payout (section A4.2). Section 4.2 says fees accrue and are paid as section 5 provides. This page gives no date and no number of days. Read section 5 before you sign up, because the Partner Agreement governs the payout.

How does Otter Quotes know a client came from me?

You get a personal referral link when you sign up as a real estate partner. A homeowner is credited to the partner whose link they clicked most recently, within 90 days, before creating their Otter Quotes account. That credit stays with that partner until 90 days after that click (section 9).

Otter Quotes' tracking records for your referral link decide attribution. Our app makes it quick and easy to send the link to your clients. If a client clicked links from more than one partner, the last click before the account was created gets the credit, and a later click does not change it while that credit lasts (sections 9.1 and 9.2). If no partner click falls within the window, the account stays unattributed. Make sure your client uses your correct, current link (section 9.5).

Here is the path for a client who comes from your link:

  1. The client opens your referral link and creates an account.
  2. The client provides a copy of an insurance estimate if there is one, purchases professional measurements of the property, or uploads measurements they already have.
  3. The client selects what needs to be quoted, such as the roof.
  4. Otter Quotes sends the scope of work to contractors, who can submit bids.
  5. The client compares the bids online, chooses one, and signs the contract online.

The referral fee depends on the client completing the job, which is the last step of that path.

What should I tell my client about the fee?

Tell your client that you are paid for the referral. Partner Agreement section 8.1 requires a partner to disclose that they are paid for referrals whenever they promote Otter Quotes, for example: "I get paid if you use my link." The disclosure has to appear in the content itself, not only in a bio or on a link page.

Do not share the fee with the client, and do not offer the client money, a gift card, a discount or any other reward for using Otter Quotes. Section 8.2 prohibits it. Do not describe the fee as a discount or a credit toward anything, which section A4.6 also rules out.

Describe the contractors only the way Otter Quotes does: they compete with bids on the same scope of work, and the client decides. Section 8.2 lists words partners must not use about contractors or about Otter Quotes, so read it before you write your own message. Do not promise a price, a saving or an outcome, and do not say what an insurer will cover (section 8.2).

Send the link to your own clients and contacts, one message at a time. Section 8.3 allows only individually written messages to named people, and no marketing text without the recipient's prior express written consent. Section 3 bars cold outreach in Otter Quotes' name. You are an independent contractor, not an Otter Quotes employee or agent, and you cannot speak for the company (section 2).

Am I allowed to accept the fee?

That depends on your employment agreement and your governing licensing agency, and Otter Quotes cannot answer it for you. Check your employment agreement and your governing licensing agency to make sure it is lawful for you to accept referral fees. Both are yours to confirm.

Do this before you sign up. Signing up costs nothing, but the question is yours to settle first. Section 7 of the Partner Agreement says the same: a partner confirms their own eligibility before accepting any fee, and Otter Quotes does not evaluate it.

Where does Otter Quotes fit?

Otter Quotes is helping after the storm; it is not a storm contractor. It creates a written scope of work for a homeowner's project and sends it to multiple contractors, who compete with bids on that same scope. The homeowner compares the bids online, next to reviews and licensing information, and signs online if they choose one. Otter Quotes does not inspect or assess damage, does not work with insurance companies, and does not sell upgrades. Contractors Compete. Homeowners Win.

Frequently asked questions

Is the referral fee a percentage of the job?

No. It is a flat dollar amount, not a percentage of anything. The terms are: "$200 when a homeowner you refer completes a project of $10,000 or more. $50 on the same terms for referrals from partners you recruit." The size of the project only decides whether the $10,000 floor is met. No fee is earned on a homeowner whose property is in a state the Partner Agreement lists as excluded (section A4.4).

Does a project of exactly $10,000 qualify?

Yes. The floor is $10,000 or more, so a project of exactly $10,000 meets it. The fee is earned when the referred homeowner completes the project and the job is approved for payout, not when a contract is signed or work is booked. Read the Partner Agreement, section A4.2, for the full terms.

What is the recruit bonus?

It is the second part of the fee sentence: "$50 on the same terms for referrals from partners you recruit." In plain terms, when an agent you personally recruited refers a homeowner who completes a qualifying project, you earn the recruit bonus on the same terms as the referral fee. It is single-level and forward-only.

Does it cost anything to become a partner?

No. You pay nothing to sign up as a partner or to refer a client. Contractors pay Otter Quotes a fee on the accepted bid. A client without an insurance estimate pays an upfront charge of as little as $22.63 for professional measurements, or can upload measurements they already have, and that charge is refunded if the client completes a job on the platform.

Does Otter Quotes tell me whether I may accept a referral fee?

No. Otter Quotes does not give legal advice and cannot tell an agent what an employment agreement or licensing rules allow. Check your employment agreement and your governing licensing agency to make sure it is lawful for you to accept referral fees.
