Why this exists

I'm an unemployed executive looking for people to build with.

I'm Dustin Stohler. I've been a lawyer, a general counsel, and a co-founder who took a company from zero to eight figures. Right now I'm between things, and I know I'm not the only one.

There are a lot of us: people with twenty-plus years of experience running teams, budgets, and companies, who are either out of work or doing work far below what we can do. The usual advice is to take whatever is offered. I'd rather build something.

We're at the point where we have all the experience and talent it takes to start from scratch. What most of us are missing is each other. Together we can build something bigger than any of us could alone.

The name comes from the phoenix. The ashes are simply the place we're rising from.

Dustin StohlerZionsville, Indiana
How it runs

Deliberately simple.

No dues.

Nobody is selling you coaching, a course, or a membership. It costs your time and nothing else.

No agenda.

We get capable people in a room and see what ideas come out of it. The best idea wins, wherever it comes from.

No owner.

I started this, but I'm not looking to run it. I have a few ideas. I'm hoping yours are better.

Who it's for

You'll fit if this sounds like you.

The goal is a group where everyone at the table has run something real, and where the work we take on makes the most of what we can contribute to our community, our families, and ourselves.

  • You have 20+ years of experience, including senior leadership.
  • You're unemployed, or employed well below your ability.
  • You would rather build something than settle for less.
  • You're close enough to Zionsville to meet in person.
  • You want partners, not a networking event.
