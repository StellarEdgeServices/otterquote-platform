Install on iPhone & iPad

Add the Partner App to your home screen in about 30 seconds — no App Store needed.

⚠️ You must use Safari for this. Chrome, Firefox, and other browsers on iPhone and iPad can't add the app to your home screen — that's an Apple restriction, not something we can change. If this page didn't open in Safari, copy the link and paste it into Safari first.
  1. 1 Make sure you're viewing this page in Safari.
  2. 2 Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up — usually at the bottom of the screen on iPhone, or the top-right on iPad)
  3. 3 Scroll down the menu that pops up and tap Add to Home Screen
  4. 4 Tap Add in the top-right corner
  5. 5 Find the OQ Partners icon on your home screen, tap it, and sign in to your partner account
