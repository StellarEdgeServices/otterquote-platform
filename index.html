How it works

Step 1

One statement of work

We create a scope of work for your job and submit it to multiple contractors.

Step 2

Contractors compete online

When contractors know they are competing for your business, they are more likely to include better materials and warranties.

Step 3

You compare and sign online

See every bid side by side, choose the one you want and sign online. Nobody sits at your kitchen table.

What you see about each contractor

  • Their bid on your statement of work
  • The materials they propose
  • The warranty they offer
  • Public reviews
  • Licensing information

We help you understand what you're offered

We will do our best to explain the costs and benefits of the products that go on your home so you can make informed decisions about what is best for you. Our goal isn't to sell you upgrades. It's to help you negotiate the best products you can for your home.

How we're paid

Contractors pay Otter Quotes a fee on the bid you accept.

You pay as little as $15 up front for professional measurements that contractors use to bid. If you complete a job on the platform, that money is refunded to you. There is no cost if you provide a copy of your insurance estimate.

What we do not do

  • We do not work with your insurance company.
  • We do not offer free inspections or assessments of your roof.
  • We do not sell upgrades.

You can change your mind

If something does not work out before work begins, you can switch to another Otter Quotes contractor at no cost, up to 3 days before your start date.

Questions

Do you work with my insurance company?

Once you have your insurance estimate, we can walk you through what it includes and what it pays for.

Will you inspect my roof?

No. We do not offer free inspections or assessments of your roof.

Do I have to hire one of the contractors?

No. There is no obligation to work with our contractors.

Who bids on my job?

You can choose from local, well-known contractors or work directly with the subcontractors who do the work.
