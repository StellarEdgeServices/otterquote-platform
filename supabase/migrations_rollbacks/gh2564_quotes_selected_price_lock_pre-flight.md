## What it does
It re-creates `public.quotes_guard_homeowner_columns()` as the gh2519 draft's body (including its live-selected-bid rule and clean-up) plus two blocks marked gh-2564, behind the apply gate described above. No function, trigger, table, policy or data change.

For client callers (`anon`/`authenticated`, not `service_role`, not an admin), on a bid whose `status` is `selected` before or after the write, a change to any of these is refused 42501: `total_price`, `fee_amount`, `fee_percentage`, `platform_fee_pct`, `platform_fee_basis`, `card_fee_cents`, `decking_price_per_sheet`, `full_redeck_price`, `per_trade_breakdown` (price and fee), and, D-381, `trade_type`, `value_adds`, `workmanship_warranty_years`, `warranty_option_id`, `warranty_snapshot`, `scope_summary` (terms).

- **Column list: the builder's proposal.** D-369 left "which columns and statuses" open. These are the eight price and fee columns named in ruling 6048924192 plus `per_trade_breakdown`, which holds the per-trade prices of a multi-trade bid. Dropping a column is deleting one line of the `IF`.
- **Status: `selected`.** `quotes_status_check` allows `draft`, `submitted`, `selected`, `declined`, `expired`; `awarded` is tested too because `apply_referral_commission()` and `switch-contractor` read it.
- **Terms columns (D-381).** The CEO named four columns (`trade_type`, `workmanship_warranty_years`, `warranty_option_id`, `value_adds`) as his translation of "trade, warranty, value-adds" and left the CTO to confirm them against the schema; all four exist on `quotes`. Two more are built in, each one line to remove: **`warranty_snapshot`** (the stored text of the warranty option; left free, it would let the warranty change under a locked `warranty_option_id`) and **`scope_summary`**. On `scope_summary` the CEO asked that it be locked only if it is what the homeowner selected on: it is. `bids.html` (`getScopeSummary`, L1211-1250) reads the start date, completion time and brand out of it for the bid card the homeowner chooses on, and `create-docusign-envelope` copies brand and estimated start date from it into the contract. `notes` is the free-text column and stays writable, as D-381 says. `warranty_document_url` and `warranty_uploaded_at` are service-side columns already refused for the contractor by the gh2519 allow-list.
- **Still writable on a selected bid:** `notes`, `auto_renew`, `supplement_acknowledged`, `fee_accepted_at`, both signature stamps. A value re-sent unchanged is not a change, so "Update Bid" with the same numbers still saves (proof S8).
- **Who can still change the price and fee of a selected bid:** `service_role` (every Edge Function, including `docusign-webhook`, whose contract-price check halts a signing when the signed price differs), admins, and owner-level `SECURITY DEFINER` functions (proof S10, S11).

## What a contractor meets
`contractor-bid-form.html` and the React bid form still open "Change Bid" on a selected bid. Until the form change of #2564 item 2 ships, a contractor who changes the price there gets the database refusal in the form's existing error alert: `quotes: the price, fee and terms of a selected bid are locked; to change them, rescind the bid and submit a new one (gh-2564)` (the words "and terms" are new in this head, for D-381; the legal read 6050041234 was of the earlier wording). That sentence reaches a customer and needs the R-177 read.

## Two things that must be true before this is applied (D-369, 6021699133 items 2 and 3)
1. **The way out must work. On the evidence here it does not.** `supabase/functions/rescind-bid/index.ts` (main, L195-200) writes `bid_status = 'rescinded'`. Production's `quotes_bid_status_check` is `CHECK (bid_status = ANY (ARRAY['active', 'expired', 'superseded', 'cancelled']))` (pg_get_constraintdef, 2026-10-08). On the throwaway copy of that constraint the function's own UPDATE, run as `service_role` on a selected bid, fails `23514 new row for relation "quotes" violates check constraint "quotes_bid_status_check"`. Every selected bid on production carries `bid_status = 'active'`, which the function's status list accepts, so the request gets as far as that UPDATE. Not tested: a real call to the deployed function. Fix the function or the constraint, and show a rescind of an `is_test` selected bid succeeding **and the rescinded bidder's re-bid being accepted by the homeowner (`rpc accept_bid` on the new bid; proof RB2 shows this works on the throwaway schema with `bid_status = 'cancelled'`, the nearest allowed value)**, before the lock is applied. **Also show, on the same `is_test` claim after the re-award, exactly one row with `status = 'selected'`** (`SELECT count(*) FROM public.quotes WHERE claim_id = <claim> AND status = 'selected'` must be 1; production's signing (`docusign-webhook`), charge (`create-payment-intent`) and completion (`mark-job-complete`) code reads the winner as `status = 'selected'` alone and fails on two rows). The proof shows this on the throwaway schema in line RP2 (rescind, re-bid, award) and RP1 (switch-contractor, hourly expiry, award); both end with `status=selected rows=1` and fail with `rows=2` on 94d01006 and on today's schema (review 6051777895 finding 1). Whoever repairs `rescind-bid` sets the constant `c_rescind_bid_writes` at the top of the gate in the draft to the value the repaired function writes. **This PR does not fix it and does not touch `rescind-bid` or the constraint** (both are outside these two drafts; the repair is the separate `env:code` change named on #2564). The lock draft therefore stays NOT APPLIED, and this is a hard precondition of its apply (CEO 6050104316: "no lock before `rescind-bid` works on production"). The proof runner cannot show the deployed function working, so it does not claim to.
2. **The bid form shows a plain message instead of "Change Bid" on a selected bid** (wording through R-177).

What a rescind after selection does to the claim (does it reopen for bids?) was left undecided by D-369.
