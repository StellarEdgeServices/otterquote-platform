The Best Agents Are Getting Ahead of Roof Non-Renewals

INDIANAPOLIS, November 11, 2026. No agent wants a client to get a renewal letter with a roof problem attached. Mediocre agents are good at apologizing when the upset calls come in. Great agents raise the conversation early and provide proactive solutions.

ACV roof schedules, roof-age limits and non-renewals mean more clients are paying for more of their roof out of pocket. A ZestyAI analysis found that ACV roof settlement schedules at the top 10 US homeowners carriers grew from 10 percent of policies in 2015 to 60 percent in 2025. If your only advice to your clients is for them to get three bids, you are failing to protect their property and their wallet.

The best insurance agents are telling their clients that winter is Roof Buying Season. When demand for roofs falls in the winter, many contractors are more willing to negotiate better prices and better materials. So great insurance agents are teaching their clients how to negotiate a great price on a new roof. They are also teaching their clients about the importance of hail-resistant shingles and labor warranties backed by the manufacturers, so a hailstorm is less likely to mean another new roof.

Don't leave your clients scrambling for contractors this spring. Talk to them now and help them get a better deal.
