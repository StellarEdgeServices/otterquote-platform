The Otter Quotes Partner App

Refer homeowners, grow your partner network, and track your referrals and payouts — right from your phone. Free, no app store needed, installs in seconds.

Works on iPhone, Android, and desktop.

What you can do

🏠

Refer Homeowners

Share your personal referral link by text, email, or QR code in one tap. Your client gets competing contractor bids — you earn $200 on completed jobs over $10,000.

🤝

Refer Partners

Know another agent, adjuster, or inspector? Share your recruit link. When partners you recruit close referrals, you earn $50 per completed job.

📊

Track Referrals & Payouts

See every referral's status and your commission history in your partner dashboard — one tap from your home screen.

How to install

 iPhone & iPad (Safari)

  1. Open this page in Safari
  2. Tap the Share button
  3. Scroll and tap Add to Home Screen
  4. Tap Add — the OQ Partners icon appears on your home screen and opens straight to your Partner Dashboard

🤖 Android (Chrome)

  1. Open this page in Chrome
  2. Tap Install the App above — or the ⋮ menu
  3. Choose Install app (or Add to Home screen)
  4. Confirm — the OQ Partners icon appears on your home screen

💻 Desktop (Chrome / Edge)

  1. Open this page in Chrome or Edge
  2. Click Install the App above — or the install icon in the address bar
  3. The app opens in its own window and pins like any program

On your computer? Scan to open on your phone.
