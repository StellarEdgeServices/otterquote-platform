4. Referral Fee Structure

Every amount under this Agreement is a flat fee. None is a percentage.

A4.1 Measurement Referral Fee: $5 for each Paid Measurement by a Referred Homeowner. The fee is earned when the purchase completes, and there is no job-value floor. A later rebate of the measurement price at job completion does not reverse it.

A4.2 Job Referral Fee: $200 when a Referred Homeowner completes a Qualifying Job and the job is approved for payout.

A4.3 Recruit Bonus: $50 when a referral by an agent Partner whom Partner personally recruited results in a Job Referral Fee that is approved for payout. This includes a Job Referral Fee the recruited Partner earns on a self-referral under Section A4.5. No Recruit Bonus is paid on a Measurement Referral Fee. Sections 4.1 (single-level, forward-only) and 4.3 (home inspectors) apply.

A4.4 Excluded states. No fee is earned on a Referred Homeowner whose property is in a state Otter Quotes lists as excluded on the partner dashboard. The list currently covers Oklahoma, Arkansas, Kansas, Nebraska and Mississippi. Otter Quotes may update the list, effective for referrals attributed after the update.

A4.5 Self-referrals. A Partner earns fees on a Paid Measurement or Qualifying Job for the Partner's own property, on the same terms as any other referral. Section 10's rules on fake homeowners and fraud still apply.

A4.6 Not a discount. Every fee is paid to Partner for making a referral. No fee is a discount, rebate, allowance or credit against any price, fee, deductible or contract amount, and no fee may be described as one.

4.1 Single-Level Recruiting and Forward-Only Accrual

The Recruit Bonus is single-level: Partner earns a Recruit Bonus only on referrals made by agents Partner personally, directly recruited into the Program. Partner does not earn any referral fee or bonus on referrals made by agents recruited by Partner's recruits, or on any further downstream recruiting chain. The Program is not a multi-level marketing structure, and no referral fee of any kind flows more than one level from the recruiting Partner to a directly-recruited agent.

The Recruit Bonus is also forward-only: it accrues only on referrals an agent submits after that agent's recruitment relationship with Partner is formally established in Otter Quotes' records. There is no retroactive credit. If an agent was already submitting referrals through the Program before being formally linked to Partner as the agent's recruiter, Partner does not earn a Recruit Bonus on any of that agent's referrals made before the recruitment link was established — only on referrals the agent makes afterward.

5. Accrual and Payment; Payment Information

5.1 Fees accrue to Partner's balance when earned. Once a month, Otter Quotes reviews the balance and approves payment by hand. An approved balance of $25 or more is paid in that month's batch. A smaller balance rolls into later months, except as Section 5.5 provides. Payment timing shown on the dashboard is an estimate, not a promise.

5.2 Payment is made through Venmo or PayPal, using the goods-and-services payment type, to the account Partner designates. No bank details are collected. Otter Quotes may designate a different payment service on notice under Section 17.

5.3 Otter Quotes pays the payment service's processing fee on each monthly payment, so Partner receives the full approved amount.

5.4 Before any payment, Partner must provide complete and current payment details. Partner must also provide any tax information Otter Quotes requests, but only where a payment is made outside the services in Section 5.2 or where a payment service or applicable law requires it. Otter Quotes does not require a Form W-9 for payments made through Venmo or PayPal. While those details are missing, or while Otter Quotes holds payment under Section 10 or 8.5, fees continue to accrue but are not paid. No interest accrues on held fees.

5.5 Balances when the partnership ends. The partnership ends when Partner quits, when Otter Quotes ends it, or after 12 months with no referral activity on Partner's account. Otter Quotes then pays the full accrued, unreversed balance in the next monthly batch after Partner's payment details are complete, even if it is under $25. Section 10 still applies.

9. Referral Tracking and Attribution

9.1 A homeowner is attributed to the last Partner whose tracked link the homeowner clicked within the 90 days before creating a Platform account.

9.2 Credit is decided when the account is created. While that credit lasts, a later click on any link does not change it. If no Partner click falls within that window when the account is created, the account stays unattributed, and no later click can claim it.

9.3 Credit lasts 90 days from the partner click. A measurement or project started within that time is credited. A measurement starts when it is purchased. A project starts when the homeowner's claim is filed on Otter Quotes. After the 90 days, the credit ends, and a measurement on a project already started earns no fee. A project started later is credited to the Partner whose tracked link the homeowner clicked most recently after the credit ended and within the 90 days before the project started; measurements on that project follow the same 90-day rule from that click. If there is no such click, no Partner is credited.

9.4 Otter Quotes' tracking records decide attribution, absent demonstrated system error. This window replaces any shorter window previously applied. It governs homeowner accounts created on or after the date version v4-2026-10 takes effect for Partner.

9.5 Recruit Bonuses are credited only on agents whom Otter Quotes' records link to Partner as Partner's recruit, under Section 4.1. Partner is responsible for making sure homeowners and recruited agents use Partner's correct, current link.

10. Reversal, Void and Offset

10.1 Otter Quotes may void an unpaid fee, or offset a paid one, if the underlying purchase or job is refunded, cancelled, charged back or reversed (the refund of the measurement price at job completion under Section A4.1 does not count); is recorded more than once; is not attributable to Partner under Section 9; or arises from fraud or a breach of this Agreement.

10.2 Refunds and chargebacks may surface late, and some refunds are not detected automatically. Otter Quotes may act under Section 10.1 within 12 months after the fee is earned. A voided amount is removed from the balance. A paid amount is offset against future fees. Except for fraud under Section 10.3, offset is Otter Quotes' sole remedy. It will not demand cash repayment of an ordinary refund, cancellation or chargeback.

10.3 Fraud. No fee is earned on a fake, fictitious or non-consenting homeowner, a purchase made with Partner's own funds for someone else, cookie stuffing, or any other manipulation of attribution. On fraud, Otter Quotes may claw back every affected fee by offset or by demand for repayment in cash, close Partner's account, and withhold unpaid fees.

15. Disputes: Individual Arbitration; No Class Actions

Read this section carefully. It covers how we resolve disputes, and it means neither of us can bring a claim as part of a class, group, or representative action — unless you opt out as described below.

Try to work it out first. Before starting arbitration or a lawsuit, send a written description of the problem to support@otterquote.com and give us 30 days to respond and try to resolve it. Most problems get solved this way.

Individual arbitration. If that doesn't resolve it, any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement or Partner's participation in the Program — other than a small claims case or an injunction request, both described below — will be resolved by binding, individual arbitration rather than in court. Arbitration is conducted under the Federal Arbitration Act, administered by JAMS under its Comprehensive Arbitration Rules and Procedures, seated in Indiana (in person, by video, or on written submissions, as the arbitrator directs). The arbitrator's decision is final and can be entered as a judgment in any court with jurisdiction.

No class actions. Claims must be brought individually, not as a plaintiff or class member in any class, consolidated, or representative action, and not on behalf of the general public. The arbitrator cannot combine more than one person's claims into one proceeding. If this no-class-action promise is found unenforceable as to a particular claim, that specific claim — and only that claim — will be decided in court instead of arbitration; every other claim still goes to individual arbitration under this Section.

Carve-outs. Either of us may bring an individual claim in small claims court instead of arbitration, if it qualifies. Either of us may also go to court to seek an injunction to stop unauthorized access, misuse of data, or infringement, without first going through arbitration or the 30-day notice above.

Partner can opt out. If Partner does not want this arbitration Section to apply, Partner must email support@otterquote.com within 30 days of the date Partner first accepts this Agreement under Section 1 — or, if Partner already had an Otter Quotes referral-partner account before this Section was added, within 30 days of the Effective Date above — with Partner's name, account email, and a clear statement that Partner is opting out of arbitration. If Partner opts out, disputes between the parties are resolved in court as described in Section 16 (Governing Law and Venue), and the rest of this Agreement still applies to Partner unchanged.

This Section is governed by the Federal Arbitration Act. If a court decides this entire Section cannot be enforced, disputes will instead be resolved in the courts described in Section 16 (Governing Law and Venue).
