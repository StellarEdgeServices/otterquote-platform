Roofing Warranty Tiers Explained: GAF and CertainTeed

A shingle warranty can mean different things depending on the product, the manufacturer's program and who installs the roof. Here are the terms, defined, with two manufacturers' own published terms side by side.

Roof paperwork can name more than one warranty. This page defines the terms you will see on a bid: manufacturer warranty, workmanship warranty, prorated, system warranty and contractor certification. It then shows how GAF and CertainTeed describe their own warranties on their own pages. Terms differ by product, so the warranty document for the product on your bid is the place to confirm them.

What Are the Two Kinds of Roof Warranty?

  • A manufacturer warranty is a promise from the shingle manufacturer about its product. CertainTeed's page states that it “warrants that its roofing shingles will be free from manufacturing defects.”
  • A workmanship warranty is a promise about the installation work rather than the product. A contractor can give one, and CertainTeed's page states: “Contractor workmanship warranties typically are for one to five years.” Some manufacturer warranties also include workmanship coverage, as the table below shows.

What Does “Lifetime” Mean in a Shingle Warranty?

GAF defines it on its warranty comparison page: “The word ‘Lifetime’ means as long as you, the original owner(s) [or the second owner(s) if coverage was properly transferred during the Smart Choice Protection Period], own the property where the shingles and/or accessories are installed.” The same page states that the Lifetime warranty “is applicable only to shingles and accessories installed on a single-family detached residence owned by individuals” and that for other owners and buildings “the length of the warranty is 40 years.” (GAF)

What Does Prorated Mean?

A prorated remedy is reduced to reflect how long the product has been in use. GAF's page defines its non-prorated period, which it calls the Smart Choice Protection Period, as the period “during which the coverage provided for in this limited warranty is non-prorated,” and states that after it, “any remedy will be reduced to reflect the use you have received from your GAF Products.” (GAF)

What Is a System Warranty, and What Is a Contractor Certification Program?

A system warranty is a warranty offered when the shingles are installed together with specified accessory products. GAF's Roofing System, System Plus, Silver Pledge and Golden Pledge warranties each list a minimum number of “Qualifying Accessory Products” that must be installed with the shingles. (GAF)

A contractor certification program is a manufacturer's program that designates contractors and states which warranties each designation can offer. GAF states: “GAF Certified™ contractors can offer one of GAF's enhanced warranties, the System Plus Limited Warranty.” “GAF Certified Plus™ contractors can offer GAF enhanced warranties including System Plus and Silver Pledge™ limited warranties.” “GAF Master Elite® contractors are entrusted to offer GAF enhanced warranties including the Golden Pledge® Limited Warranty.” (GAF) CertainTeed’s own program wording is in the table below.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a “lifetime” shingle warranty mean?

GAF defines the word on its warranty comparison page, and the definition is quoted in the Lifetime section above. Other manufacturers publish their own definitions.

Which warranties depend on who installs the roof?

The table above gives, for each warranty, who the manufacturer's page says can offer it. GAF names a different contractor designation for each of its enhanced warranties, and CertainTeed ties SureStart PLUS to an Integrity Roof System installed by a CertainTeed Credentialed contractor.

Is the manufacturer warranty the same as the contractor's warranty?

No. A manufacturer warranty is the manufacturer's promise about its product, and a workmanship warranty is a promise about the installation. Some manufacturer warranties also include workmanship coverage. GAF's page lists workmanship as not covered under its Shingle & Accessory and System Plus warranties and as covered under Silver Pledge and Golden Pledge.

Can a roof warranty be transferred when I sell my home?

It depends on the warranty. GAF's definition of Lifetime, quoted above, refers to second owners and to transfer during the Smart Choice Protection Period. The CertainTeed SureStart row above quotes its page on transfer during the SureStart period. CertainTeed's limited warranty states that it can be transferred once, by the original owner to the first subsequent owner, and that a transfer after the SureStart period is limited to two years from the date of transfer (CertainTeed, Asphalt Shingle Residential Products 2025 Limited Warranty, which applies to shingles installed in 2025). Read the document for your product for its transfer terms.

What does non-prorated mean?

It means the remedy is not reduced for the time the product has been in use. GAF calls its non-prorated period the Smart Choice Protection Period; its definition is quoted in the prorated section above.
