This page defines five terms that come up when an insurer is involved in a roof repair: appraisal clause, independent adjuster, insurance adjuster, public adjuster and re-inspection.

Adjuster Terms

Appraisal clause

A policy may include an appraisal clause, which describes a way to resolve a disagreement about the amount of a loss. Source: Texas Department of Insurance, Home Insurance Guide.

Independent adjuster

The National Association of Insurance Commissioners (NAIC) glossary defines an independent adjuster as a "freelance contractor paid a fee for adjusting losses on behalf of companies". Source: NAIC Glossary of Insurance Terms.

Insurance adjuster

An insurance adjuster represents the insurer and prepares the insurer's estimate. Source: Maryland Insurance Administration, Public Adjuster.

Public adjuster

A public adjuster (PA) is someone a homeowner hires to work on the homeowner's claim, rather than for the insurer. Licensing rules are set by each state; your state's Department of Insurance can tell you what applies. An insurance adjuster works for the insurer. Source: Texas Department of Insurance, Home Insurance Guide.

Re-inspection

A re-inspection is a term for a second inspection of a property after a first claim inspection.
