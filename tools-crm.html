Put it on your phone in 30 seconds

There's no app store download. OQ CRM installs straight from your browser and then behaves exactly like any other app — its own icon, full screen, no address bar.

iPhone & iPad

Use Safari
  1. Open Safari and go to crm.otterquote.com. (This step needs Safari — other iPhone browsers hide the option.)
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen — the square with an arrow pointing up.
  3. Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add in the top right corner.
  5. The OQ CRM icon is now on your home screen. Open it from there from now on.
If you don't see "Add to Home Screen," scroll further down the share menu — on some iPhones it sits below the row of apps, under "Edit Actions."

Android

Use Chrome
  1. Open Chrome and go to crm.otterquote.com.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu in the top right corner.
  3. Tap Install app — or Add to Home screen if that's what your phone calls it.
  4. Tap Install to confirm.
  5. The OQ CRM icon lands on your home screen and in your app drawer.
On a Samsung phone using Samsung Internet: tap the three lines at the bottom right, then Add page to → Home screen.

Make it the first thing you see

If you'd rather have OQ CRM open every time you open your browser, set it as your home page. Here's how, on the browsers contractors actually use.

Chrome on Android

Tap the three-dot menu → Settings → Homepage. Switch it On, choose Enter custom web address, and type crm.otterquote.com. The home button in Chrome now opens OQ CRM.

iPhone & iPad

Safari on iPhone doesn't have a home page setting — Apple doesn't offer one. The home screen icon from the steps above is the equivalent, and it's actually better: it opens full screen with no browser bar in the way.

Chrome on a computer

Click the three-dot menu → Settings → Appearance, turn on Show home button, and enter crm.otterquote.com. To have it open automatically when Chrome starts, go to Settings → On startup → Open a specific page and add the same address.

Microsoft Edge

Click the three-dot menu → Settings → Start, home, and new tabs. Under the home button section, choose Enter URL and type crm.otterquote.com.

Safari on a Mac

Open OQ CRM in a tab, then go to Safari → Settings → General and click Set to Current Page next to Homepage. Set "New windows open with" to Homepage.
