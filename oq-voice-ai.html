Where we plan to draw the line

Putting AI on the phone with your customers is a real risk if it's done carelessly. Here's what we're building toward for every manager in this pilot.

A person always confirms the work

Diagnoses and parts lists are drafts for your team, never instructions to a homeowner. Nothing gets ordered, promised, or scheduled without one of your people signing off.

It hands off when it should

An angry customer, a safety issue, anything outside the routine call — it's built to stop and get a human involved rather than push through.

No pricing surprises

Pricing isn't set until the pilot wraps, and pilot members will know the number before anything is billed — not after.
